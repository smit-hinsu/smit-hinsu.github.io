About

I am a systems thinker and an impact-driven software engineer with over a decade at Google building and optimizing large-scale ML systems. I'm endlessly curious about how the world works at every level — from physics to the social sciences — and I love understanding how the pieces connect rather than seeing them in isolation.

Above all, I'm a mentor at heart: I get real joy from helping others grow. I care about scaling impact not just through code, but by leading cross-functional initiatives, mentoring engineers, and fostering collaborative, efficiency-focused engineering cultures.

Goal To understand intelligence and the nature of the universe.
